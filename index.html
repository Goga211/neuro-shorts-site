Short science videos, produced with care

Neuro Shorts is a private, single-user tool that helps an independent developer prepare short science videos and publish them to their own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Prepares vertical science videos with narration and subtitles.
  • Uploads each video to the developer's own YouTube channel after the developer manually reviews and approves it.
  • Reads aggregate statistics of the developer's own videos to decide which topics to cover next.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy for how YouTube data is handled, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Neuro Shorts is not a public service: it does not offer sign-up and is used only by its developer.
