Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Neuro Shorts ("the application") is a private tool operated by an independent developer to publish videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • Upload access (scope youtube.upload) to publish videos to the authorized channel.
  • Read-only channel data (scope youtube.readonly) such as the channel and its video identifiers.
  • Aggregate analytics (scope yt-analytics.readonly): views, average view percentage, likes and subscribers gained for the channel's own videos.

The application does not access data of any other YouTube users or channels.

How we use and store data

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer and are never shared.
  • Video identifiers and aggregate statistics are stored locally to choose future video topics. Statistics are refreshed daily while a video is being tracked and are automatically deleted 30 days after they were last fetched, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • To suggest future video topics, the titles of the developer's own videos and their aggregate statistics (views, average view percentage, subscribers gained) are sent to an AI language model provider (Anthropic Claude, accessed through KIE AI). No personal data of YouTube users and no OAuth tokens are sent.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising. Apart from the AI provider above, no data is shared with third parties.
  • The application does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Because the application is used only by its developer, locally stored channel data is deleted by the developer when access is revoked.
